  How much testing does a .NET application need, and where should you spend the effort?
  We'll cover unit and integration tests through practical C# examples, discuss common testing mistakes, and look at tools such as WireMock, Testcontainers, Respawn and others. We'll explain where Microsoft.Testing.Platform fits in the .NET tooling and how to choose testing strategies for your project. We'll also discuss using AI to write and maintain tests, and how to judge whether those tests are useful.
